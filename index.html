    What the real version needs

    • Storage bucket for raw uploads and Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs (S3, R2 or GCS)
    • A processing worker with GDAL / PDAL that reads BAG, reprojects and builds tiles and contours
    • Tile hosting: Mapbox Tiling Service, or a COG tiler such as TiTiler behind a CDN
    • Sign-in for admins and a small database listing datasets
